INDEPENDENT MATH CHECK

Edge is not the same as EV.

A mean above a prop line does not tell us the probability of winning.

Assumed win probability 55%Assumed push probability 0%Hypothetical decimal odds 1.91
Expected net return / unit staked{{EV}}
EV = p(win) × (odds − 1) − p(loss)

Probability is an assumption, not a forecast. No market prices, real props, or wagering integrations.

Read the model assumptions & limits+

Baseline: take the latest six events whose outcomes were complete and available before the decision time; weight oldest to newest by 0.8⁵ … 0.8⁰. Require at least three observations. Optimize the unrounded means, not the displayed rounded values.

Quality gates: explicit timezones; unique IDs; finite numeric values; valid status; consistent game times; a fixture snapshot no older than 24 hours. These thresholds are project choices, not measured provider SLAs.

Boundaries: at most 32 players and three lineups; a bounded search stops without an “optimal” output if its node budget is exceeded. No sport-specific scoring conversion, news model, trained usage model, real-data backtest or calibrated uncertainty is delivered. Our historical-point mean is not LineStar’s stated median.
